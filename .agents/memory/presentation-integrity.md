---
name: Presentation integrity
description: Why the animated order replay must remain separate from the historical strategy results.
---

Keep reported strategy returns separate from the interactive teaching simulation. Label its tape and calendar as fictional and compressed; never describe its executions as historical fills.

**Why:** The source repository bundles frozen results but not the underlying raw market tapes. A teaching animation can explain order mechanics without falsely claiming to reproduce those results.

**How to apply:** Use the original deck and frozen repository tables for result claims. Use explicit illustrative labels on synthetic orders, demo fee assumptions and date-compressed timelines. A future true historical replay requires verified source tapes.
